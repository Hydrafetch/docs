There are two ways to get a logo:

| You want | Use | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A logo to show on a web page | `https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/{domain}` in an `<img>` tag | Your plan's logo allowance, not credits |
| A logo's URL as data, from your server | `GET /v1/web/brand/logo?domain=...` | 1 credit |

## Logos in your pages

`https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/{domain}` returns the company's logo as an image, not as JSON. It comes from the same work behind [brand data](/concepts/brand), cut down to the one thing most people want.

```html
<img src="https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/stripe.com?token=hf_pk_your_publishable_key" alt="Stripe" />
```

Two things about it are different from every other endpoint, on purpose.

<Card title="Logo images never use credits" icon="/icons/coins.svg">
  They count against a separate allowance in your plan. A page showing forty logos uses forty logo serves and zero credits.
</Card>

<Card title="The key goes in your page" icon="/icons/key.svg">
  An `<img>` tag cannot send a header, so this endpoint takes a **publishable key** in the URL. Publishable keys are meant to be public, and they work here and nowhere else.
</Card>

## Publishable keys

Create one in the dashboard next to your secret keys. They start with `hf_pk_`, and it is safe to commit them and put them in browser HTML.

A publishable key **cannot** call `/v1/web/scrape`, `/v1/web/crawl` or any other endpoint; those answer `401`. It only serves logos, which is why it is safe to publish.

You can lock a key to a list of domains. Requests are then checked against their `Referer` or `Origin` header, and anything else is refused.

<Warning>
  **Domain locking is not access control.** The browser sets `Referer`, so anyone can send any value. Locking stops someone casually reusing a key copied from your page, which is useful, but it will not stop someone determined.

  It also does not apply to images already cached at the edge. Once a URL has been served to an allowed domain, that exact URL is served from cache without the check, for up to 24 hours. For the same reason, revoking a key does not instantly stop URLs that are already cached.

  Your allowance and our abuse detection are what really limit misuse.
</Warning>

If you lock a key, make sure your pages send a referrer. A `Referrer-Policy` of `no-referrer`, or `<img referrerpolicy="no-referrer">`, gets every request refused.

## Options

<ParamField query="token" type="string" required>
  Your publishable key. From a server, you can send it in the `X-API-Key` header instead.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="size" default="128" type="number">
  The longest side in pixels, from 16 to 512. Rounded up to the nearest size we store.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="theme" default="light" type="string">
  The background you are drawing on: `light`, `dark` or `auto`. `light` and `dark` fix the logo to one colour. `auto` follows the viewer's operating system setting. Read [Getting the theme right](#getting-the-theme-right) before using `auto`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="type" default="icon" type="string">
  `icon` for the square mark, `wordmark` for the full logo with the name. If the company has only one, you get that one.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="fallback" default="monogram" type="string">
  What to serve when we have no logo: `monogram` (the first letter in the brand's colour), `transparent` (a 1 by 1 clear pixel) or `404`.
</ParamField>

## Examples

```html Dark background
<img src="https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/figma.com?token=hf_pk_your_key&theme=dark&size=64" alt="Figma" />
```

```html The full wordmark
<img src="https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/vercel.com?token=hf_pk_your_key&type=wordmark" alt="Vercel" />
```

```html Remove the image when there is no logo
<img src="https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/example.com?token=hf_pk_your_key&fallback=404" alt="" onerror="this.remove()" />
```

## Getting the theme right

Most logos are one colour, and the wrong one is invisible, not just wrong. A white logo on a white card looks to your users as if there is no logo at all.

**Pass the theme your page is actually showing,** not the operating system's:

```html
<img src="https://img.hydrafetch.com/logo/github.com?token=hf_pk_your_key&theme=light" alt="GitHub" />
```

Use `theme=auto` only in one case. An adaptive logo decides its colour inside the image, and an image in an `<img>` tag cannot see your page, only the viewer's operating system setting. If your site always follows the operating system, `auto` is right. **If your site has its own light and dark switch, `auto` is wrong whenever a visitor sets your switch differently from their system,** which is exactly when a missing logo is most noticeable.

So with a theme switch, pass `light` or `dark` yourself. The React package takes the theme once on its provider and every `<Logo>` inside follows it:

```tsx
import { HydrafetchProvider, Logo } from "@hydrafetch/react";

<HydrafetchProvider publishableKey="hf_pk_your_key" theme={resolvedTheme}>
  <Logo domain="github.com" />
</HydrafetchProvider>
```

See [SDKs](/sdks#in-the-browser) for the browser packages.

## A domain we have never seen

The first request for a new domain looks it up there and then and waits, instead of answering with a placeholder. A placeholder on the first view would look like "they have no logo" when we are seconds away from having one.

The wait has a limit. Past it, the lookup finishes in the background, that one request gets the fallback, and the next request is served from storage. Expect a new domain to take a few seconds the first time and every request after to be instant.

## Caching

A logo is served with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400`, so browsers and CDNs keep it for a day. Fallbacks are cached for much less time, so a domain we resolve later starts showing its real logo on its own.

Because logos are cached at the edge, most requests never reach us, and anything served from cache uses none of your allowance.

## A logo as data

From your server, `GET /v1/web/brand/logo` returns the logo's details as JSON, including a public image URL you can store and show anywhere. It costs 1 credit, compared with 5 for the full [brand record](/concepts/brand).

```bash
curl "https://api.hydrafetch.com/v1/web/brand/logo?domain=stripe.com&theme=dark" \
  -H "X-API-Key: hf_your_key_here"
```

It takes `domain` (a URL works too), `theme` and `type`, with the same meanings as above.

## Billing

Logo images count against your plan's logo allowance for each billing cycle, **separately from credits**. A logo image never uses a credit, and credits never buy logo images.

Only a delivered logo counts. If we have no logo, or are still looking it up, you get a placeholder and your allowance is not touched. Neither is anything served from the edge cache.

Going over your allowance does not break your pages straight away. For **7 days** we keep answering with monogram placeholders instead of errors, so nothing on your site turns into a broken image while you notice and upgrade. After that the endpoint returns an error. See [Plans and billing](/help/billing).
