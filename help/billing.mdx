Every call is priced in credits, and a page costs the same whatever it took to read. Failed calls are never billed. Every price is in [Credits & billing](/concepts/credits).

## The free start

New workspaces get 500 free credits that last 30 days, with no card needed. When they run out or expire, choose a plan to keep going.

## Choosing a plan

Plans differ in the credits included each month, the overage price, the request rate limit, how many people can join the workspace, and the logo allowance. Every endpoint is on every plan.

The [pricing page](https://hydrafetch.com/pricing/) has a slider. Enter how many pages you expect to read each month and it shows the plan that fits and its price.

If you are unsure, start smaller than you think. Upgrading takes effect as soon as it is paid, so being on too small a plan for a week costs very little. Being on too large a plan for a year does not.

## When a plan's credits run out

On a paid plan nothing stops mid-job. Calls carry on at your plan's overage price, billed per 10,000 credits, and each plan's overage price is on the pricing page.

Overage always costs more per credit than your plan and more than the plan above it. That is on purpose: if you are often in overage, upgrading is cheaper.

## Changing plans

| Change | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Upgrade | You pay the difference for the rest of the period, and the bigger allowance and limits start once that payment goes through. |
| Downgrade | Nothing is refunded. You keep the credits and limits you paid for until the period renews, and the lower price starts from the next invoice. |
| Cancel | Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then stops. |

## Invoices and payment

Payments and subscriptions are handled by [Kelviq](https://kelviq.com), our payment provider. Invoices arrive by email and are also in the dashboard. We never store full card numbers.

## Refunds

If a plan does not suit you after your first charge, email [team@hydrafetch.com](mailto:team@hydrafetch.com) within **7 days of that charge** and we will review a refund.

After the first paid month we do not refund past periods, and overage is not refundable because it pays for capacity you already used. The full policy, including your legal rights, is on the [refund policy](https://hydrafetch.com/refund-policy/) page.

## Large volumes

Past a million credits a month, the published plans stop being the cheapest option. We then price against your real volume, with custom rate limits, invoicing, terms and whatever your security review needs.
